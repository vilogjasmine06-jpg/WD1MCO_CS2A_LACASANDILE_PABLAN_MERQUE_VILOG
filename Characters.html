THE BOSS BABY

The Boss Baby, also known as Theodore Lindsey Templeton, is a smart and confident baby who works as a secret agent for Baby Corp. He wears a suit and tie and acts like a business executive.

TIM TEMPLETON

Tim Templeton is a seven-year-old boy with a big imagination and a loving heart. He initially feels jealous of his younger brother, but eventually becomes his loyal partner and best friend.

Francis E. Francis is the main antagonist of The Boss Baby. He is the former CEO of Puppy Co. who is jealous of babies and wants puppies to become more loved than them.

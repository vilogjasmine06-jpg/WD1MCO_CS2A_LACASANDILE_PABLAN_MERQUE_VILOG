SYNOPSIS

The Boss Baby is an animated comedy about a suit-wearing baby who arrives at the Templeton home carrying a briefcase and talking like a boss. His 7-year-old brother, Tim, is suspicious of him and soon discovers the baby is actually a secret agent from Baby Corp. Together, the brothers must team up to stop Puppy Co. from launching a new puppy that steals all the love from babies. As they battle the villain, Francis E. Francis, they learn to trust each other along the way. Ultimately, it is a heartwarming comedy-adventure about family, teamwork, and overcoming sibling rivalry.
